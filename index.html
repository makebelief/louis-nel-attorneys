WELCOME TO THE FIRM

Louis Nel Attorneys / Prokureurs.

Legal support founded on experience and practical guidance.

Based in Culemborg Park, Randfontein, the firm assists people and businesses in civil litigation, criminal matters, RAF claims, family law, commercial disputes and related issues.

Director Louis Nel was admitted as an attorney in 2004 and has held the right of appearance in the High Courts since 2009. The practice also accepts correspondent instructions from legal colleagues.
